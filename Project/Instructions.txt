                    __MUFU__ 

                --INSTRUCTIONS--


            _MAIN COMMANDS FOR GAME_

look - Look around your current location.

move - Move to another location.

collect - Collect an item that is available in your current location.

item - Inspect an item in your inventory.

give - Give an item to 'IT' when you are at the temple.

list - Check your bucket list.

pray - Pray to IT when you are ready to finish the game.

lopeta - Quit the game.


                --OBSERVATION--
 when you type the command -look- it will give you the information about your surroundings
 and also inform you of any items that you can collect using command -collect-.
 There is a location where you will need another item to advance through the game,
 you will get a clue about it if you -look- around.


                 --MOVEMENT--
 You will start off inside the cabin.
 When you are outside the cabin, you can choose a direction: 
 - north
 - west
 - east 
 - cabin 
 - south
 
   if you are in the cabin either in the beginning or at another point of the game,
   the Menu command - move will first change your location to the outside, only then
   will you be able to choose the other directions.

   if you are either in north, south, east or west and want to go somewhere else
   then when you type the command move, it will only give an option of -back-
   which beings the player to the outside.


                 --INVENTORY--
 
 Collected items are stored in your bag. Some items may be useful to advance
    in the game, so think carefully before giving an item to 'IT'.

                  --SAVING--

 Your game can be saved so that you can continue to play later. Your saved game
 contains information such as your name, location, inventory, and other
 relevant game information.

 When you start the game again, you can choose to continue a saved game
 or start a new game.
